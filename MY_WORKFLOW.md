# My workflow settings

Edit these defaults before starting a new film. A specific request in chat overrides these defaults.

- Mode: plan-only
- Story idea: choose an original everyday kindness story
- Audience: beginner English learners
- English level: A1-A2
- Learning goal: polite requests and everyday action words
- Target vocabulary: help, please, carry, find, thank you
- Visual style: warm, colorful 3D animated family film
- Image Type: 3D
- Ratio: landscape 16:9
- Duration: 60 seconds
- Character A: choose one fictional adult; lock appearance and wardrobe
- Character B: choose one fictional child; lock appearance and wardrobe
- Voice A: warm adult voice, clear Australian English, gentle delivery
- Voice B: bright child voice, clear Australian English, gentle delivery
- Captions: none
- Music: none
- Batch size: 5 (reduce to the account's observed limit)
- Corrections per asset: at most 2
- Export: FullHD MP4 in VideoExpress, no automatic download or publication

Use production mode when you want the agent to operate VideoExpress. Plan-only mode writes the story and prompts without generating media or spending credits. Keep exactly two characters and 4-5 second clips in this starter. Other cast sizes, captions, music or services require an explicit workflow revision.
